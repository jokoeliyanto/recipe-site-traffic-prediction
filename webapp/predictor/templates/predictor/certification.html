How this project relates to the certification

This project is my submission for the practical exam of the DataCamp {{ certificate.title }} Certification. DataCamp provides the business scenario and the Recipe Site Traffic dataset; the data validation, analysis, modelling, presentation and recommendations are my own work.

The practical exam asks candidates to:

  • Validate and clean every column of a real-world dataset.
  • Explore the data with charts and describe the findings.
  • Fit a baseline model and a comparison model, then evaluate them.
  • Define a business metric and estimate its current value.
  • Present the results and recommendations to a non-technical stakeholder.

Passing the exam completed the certification. This web app takes the final model one step further, turning it into a tool the product team could use every day.
