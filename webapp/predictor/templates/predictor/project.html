Which recipes should go on the homepage?

Tasty Bytes, a recipe and meal-plan subscription business, features one recipe on its homepage every day. A popular pick lifts traffic to the rest of the site by up to 40%, and more traffic means more subscriptions. This app answers the product team's request with a model that predicts which recipes will drive high traffic.

FromProduct Manager, Recipe Discovery
ToHead of Data Science
SubjectCan you help us predict popular recipes?

The product manager picks a favourite recipe from a selection each day and has no reliable way to tell whether it will be popular. They asked the data science team to:

  1. Predict which recipes will lead to high traffic.
  2. Correctly predict high-traffic recipes 80% of the time.

They also asked for a recommendation on what to do next, whatever the results.

FromHead of Data Science
SubjectNew project from the product team

The brief to the data scientist:

  • Predict popular recipes 80% of the time and minimise the chance of showing unpopular ones.
  • Validate the data, because it might not match its description.
  • Write a report that explains every decision, and present the findings to the product team in 8–10 slides.
